AppReleaseHelper Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 20, 2026 · Applies to AppReleaseHelper (Bundle ID: com.CZS.AppReleaseHelper)

1. Summary

AppReleaseHelper is a macOS utility that runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, upload, sell, or share personal data through the app. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or cross-app tracking SDKs.

2. Data processed by the app

The app processes the following content on your Mac:

  • Project information, questionnaire answers, and checklist progress you create.
  • Privacy-policy form content and distribution configuration you enter.
  • Images, Xcode projects, offline JSON packs, and export locations you explicitly select.
  • A macOS security-scoped bookmark used to reopen an Xcode project you selected.

This content is used only to provide app functionality. It remains on your device and is not sent to the developer or any third party.

3. Network, analytics, and tracking

AppReleaseHelper:

  • Makes no network requests.
  • Creates no user account.
  • Contains no advertising, analytics, or telemetry.
  • Does not read advertising identifiers, device serial numbers, or identifiers used for cross-app tracking.
  • Integrates no third-party SDKs.

Reference data is updated only when you explicitly select and import a local JSON file.

4. File access

The app uses macOS system file panels to request access to files or folders you select, including to:

  • Read screenshot or App Icon source images.
  • Inspect a selected .xcodeproj.
  • Export screenshots, App Icons, Markdown documents, or scripts.
  • Import offline reference packs.

App Sandbox limits the app's access. The app cannot freely browse other files unless you explicitly select or authorize them.

5. Local storage, retention, and deletion

App data is stored in the macOS sandbox container for Bundle ID com.CZS.AppReleaseHelper, primarily at:

~/Library/Containers/com.CZS.AppReleaseHelper/Data/Library/Preferences/
~/Library/Containers/com.CZS.AppReleaseHelper/Data/Library/Application Support/AppReleaseHelper/

Data remains until you delete the corresponding project in the app or remove the app container. To erase all data, quit and uninstall the app, then delete the sandbox container above. Files you exported must be removed separately from their chosen locations.

6. Sharing and third parties

Because the app does not collect or upload data, we do not share project information or files with third parties. Apple or other reference links are opened by you in your web browser, and those websites are responsible for their own privacy practices.

7. Children's privacy

AppReleaseHelper is intended for software developers and is not directed specifically at children. The app does not collect personal data from children or other users.

8. Policy changes

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date and, where applicable, update the App Privacy responses in App Store Connect.
