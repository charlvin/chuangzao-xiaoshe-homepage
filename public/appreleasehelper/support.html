FAQ

Why does Project Doctor ask me to select an .xcodeproj?

The app runs in a sandbox and can read a project only after you grant access through a macOS system panel. The selected project is checked locally and is never uploaded.

How do I delete local data?

Delete projects in the app first. To remove all AppReleaseHelper data, quit and uninstall the app, then delete:

~/Library/Containers/com.CZS.AppReleaseHelper/

Files you exported must be removed separately from their chosen locations.

How do I update rejection and policy references?

The app does not connect to the network. After obtaining a JSON pack published by the maintainer, use the in-app import action to select it manually. Use Restore Built-in to roll back.

Where can I download the app?

AppReleaseHelper is distributed only through the Mac App Store. The product page will show the official App Store link when available.
