Daily Saying Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Daily Saying iOS and iPadOS app and its Home Screen widget, collectively referred to as the “App.” The developer and relevant data controller is Chuangzao Xiaoshe (Suzhou) Information Technology Co., Ltd.

1. Our core approach

The App is designed to be local first. It requires no account, contains no advertising, and uses no third-party analytics or cross-app tracking services. Based on the features in the current version, no personal data is collected by, uploaded to, sold by, or shared through servers operated by the developer.

2. Information processed on your device

The App processes and stores the following information on your device:

  • Quotes, excerpts, reminders, and other text that you type, paste, or import.
  • Dates, time periods, repeat options, and enabled status associated with your content.
  • Preferences such as theme, text size, language, and nighttime messages.
  • Local data required for the app and widget to show the currently scheduled item.

This information is stored in the App's local container and the App Group container shared by the app and widget. It is not automatically sent to the developer.

3. Files and photos you choose

Text files

When you choose a text file to import, the App reads that selected file to parse and create content locally on your device. The App does not access files that you have not selected.

Background images

When you use the system photo picker to choose a background for a quote card, the App receives only the image you explicitly select and processes it locally for preview and card generation. This does not give the App ongoing read access to your entire photo library.

Saving to Photos

The App requests permission to add a generated image to your photo library only when you choose Save to Photos. Denying this permission does not affect core browsing, scheduling, or widget functionality.

4. Diagnostic information

The App may keep limited diagnostic logs locally on your device to troubleshoot startup, database, or scheduling errors. Diagnostic logs are not uploaded automatically. The developer receives information only if you contact support and choose to provide it.

If you contact support by email, your email address, message, and attachments are processed by your chosen email provider and used only to handle the support request. Do not send sensitive information unrelated to the issue.

5. Data sharing and third-party services

The current version does not integrate advertising networks, third-party analytics SDKs, third-party sign-in, or a developer-operated cloud data service. It does not sell information to data brokers.

When you use the system share sheet, photo picker, file picker, device backup, or email features, related processing is performed by Apple or the third-party app or service you choose under their respective privacy policies.

6. Retention and deletion

  • You can delete content and related records you added from within the App.
  • Uninstalling the App removes data in its local and shared containers, although historical copies may remain in operating-system backups.
  • Device and iCloud backup retention, restoration, and deletion are controlled by your system settings and Apple's applicable policies.
  • Because the developer does not operate a server that stores App content, the developer generally cannot remotely access, restore, or delete data stored locally on your device.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed primarily to children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. Because users provide or import their own content, parents or guardians should supervise use when appropriate.

8. Security

The App relies on the iOS application sandbox and App Group mechanisms to store data. No storage method can guarantee absolute security. Avoid storing or sending highly sensitive information such as passwords or identity numbers in quotes or support emails.

9. Changes to this policy

If the App's data practices change materially, this policy will be updated with a new effective date and revised content. Notice may also be provided through the public page, App release notes, or an appropriate in-app method.
