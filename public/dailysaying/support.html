Quick start

Set up Daily Saying

  1. Open the Content Library and add, paste, or import text.
  2. Choose the start date, end date, and display period.
  3. Return to the home view to confirm the currently scheduled item.
  4. Touch and hold the system Home Screen and add the Daily Saying widget.

Frequently asked questions

The widget is not showing the latest content

  • Open the app once and confirm that its home view shows the expected item.
  • Check that the content is enabled and its schedule includes the current time.
  • Make sure iOS has not offloaded the app or restricted its background activity.
  • Remove the widget from the Home Screen and add it again.
  • Restart the device if the issue continues.

Widget updates are scheduled by iOS. The app supplies a timeline, but the system controls the exact refresh time, so a change may not occur at the precise second of a boundary.

The app shows unexpected content

  • Verify the device date, time, and time zone.
  • Check the Timeline for a schedule that covers the current time.
  • Confirm that the content is enabled and its date and time period are correct.
  • If schedules overlap, adjust their time ranges and check again.

How do I import multiple items?

Choose an import option in the Content Library, then paste text or select a text file. You can choose a splitting method and preview the detected items before importing. UTF-8 plain-text files with clear line breaks or blank lines work best.

Why can't I save a quote card?

Allow Daily Saying to add images to your photo library when saving a card. If you previously denied access, review the app's Photos permission in System Settings. You can also use the system share sheet to save the card elsewhere.

Does my data sync between devices?

Daily Saying currently has no account system or in-app cloud sync. Data is stored locally on the current device. Whether it is included in a device or iCloud backup depends on your system backup settings.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete content and records you added from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Historical copies may remain in system backups subject to Apple's backup settings and retention behavior.
