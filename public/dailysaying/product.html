Daily Saying

Meaningful words, exactly when you want them

Daily Saying is a local-first text and widget app built around scheduled display. Add a quote, reminder, excerpt, or any words you want to revisit, then choose when they should appear. The app and Home Screen widget automatically show the content scheduled for the current time.

Key features

Automatic scheduling

Arrange content by date and time period, then let the current item appear automatically.

Home Screen widget

See the current quote, reminder, or excerpt without opening the app.

Flexible input

Add items manually, paste text, or import text files and preview detected items.

Batch organization

Split longer text into individual items and schedule them together.

Timeline

Review past and upcoming scheduled content in one place.

Quote cards

Select a background image and create a card locally to save or share.

Made for

  • A favorite quote each day.
  • Study excerpts and work reminders.
  • Book notes, poems, and personal reflections on the Home Screen.
  • Different content for mornings, work hours, and bedtime.

Local first

Daily Saying requires no account, contains no advertising, and uses no third-party analytics or tracking services. Content, schedules, and preferences are processed only on the device and in the local container shared by the app and its widget.
